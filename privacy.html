Privacy Policy

EPH Technologies Limited · Last updated: 28 October 2026 · Effective: 28 October 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how GroupYetu360, operated by EPH Technologies Limited, collects, uses, stores and protects your personal data. By using GroupYetu360, you agree to the terms described here. This policy complies with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

1. Who We Are

GroupYetu360 is a community management platform operated by EPH Technologies Limited, a company incorporated in Kenya (Registration No. PVT-RXUMLM2Q), with offices at Western Heights, 8th Floor, Karuna Road, Westlands, Nairobi.

For all privacy matters, contact us at: info@groupyetu.org

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Full name, email address, phone number and ID number (for member registration)
  • Organisation name, registration number and contact details (for group administrators)
  • Financial information including contribution amounts, payment references and bank balance figures entered by administrators
  • Meeting records, welfare event details and project information
  • Messages and communications sent through the platform's SMS feature
  • Identity verification: photos of the front and back of your national ID or passport, a photo of you holding it, and your name and ID number. We ask for these only when you start a Michango, organise an event as a person or business, or become an official of a group that collects money by M-Pesa through GroupYetu360
  • Event organisers that are businesses: business name, registration number and, where given, KRA PIN
  • What you publish: the title, story and photo of a Michango, and the details of an event you create. These pages are public, together with the organiser's name and the amount raised or tickets available
  • Payout details: the M-Pesa number, paybill or till to which Michango or event money is to be sent

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Login activity and session data
  • Device type, browser type and IP address
  • Pages visited and features used within the platform
  • Error logs and performance data

2.3 Information from third parties

  • Google Sign-In: If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Safaricom (M-Pesa): When you pay or receive money through GroupYetu360, we receive the payment confirmation, including the M-Pesa receipt number, amount, date, phone number and the name M-Pesa holds for the account. We use the name to confirm that payouts reach the right person. We never receive your M-Pesa PIN.
  • Africa's Talking: When you buy airtime or data, we receive the delivery status for the number you topped up.
  • Celcom Africa: SMS delivery status and recipient data for bulk messages sent through the platform.

2.4 People who give or buy tickets without an account

Anyone can give to a Michango or buy an event ticket without signing up. In that case we collect only the phone number used to pay, the name M-Pesa returns for it, and any name you choose to give, so we can record the payment, thank you or send your tickets by SMS.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data only for the following purposes:

  • To operate and provide the GroupYetu360 platform to you and your organisation
  • To authenticate your identity and manage your account, including two-factor authentication where enabled
  • To process and record financial contributions and payments
  • To send SMS notifications you or your administrator have requested, and SMS receipts, thank-you messages and tickets
  • To verify the identity of Michango and event organisers and of group officials, to protect givers, buyers and members from fraud
  • To publish the Michango and event pages that organisers create, and to check tickets at the door
  • To provide customer support and respond to your enquiries
  • To improve the platform through analysis of aggregate usage patterns
  • To comply with legal obligations under Kenyan law

We do not sell your personal data to any third party. We do not use your data for advertising.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase infrastructure, which uses PostgreSQL databases hosted on AWS with encryption at rest and in transit (TLS 1.2+). Supabase is SOC 2 Type 2 certified.

We implement the following security measures:

  • Row-level security (RLS) ensuring organisations can only access their own data
  • Role-based access control - members see only their personal data; admins manage their organisation only
  • Optional two-factor authentication (2FA) for admin, treasurer and superadmin roles - a one-time verification code is generated and emailed to your registered account email via our email provider, Resend, and expires after 5 minutes
  • Identity photos are kept in private storage that only our trust and safety staff can open, through links that expire after five minutes, and every view is logged
  • All passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • API keys and credentials are encrypted at the database level
  • HTTPS enforced on all connections

5. Data Sharing

We share your data only with the following parties, strictly to provide our service:

  • Supabase Inc. - Database and authentication infrastructure provider
  • Safaricom PLC - M-Pesa payment prompts, payouts and payment confirmations - see our Terms of Service for how payments work
  • Africa's Talking - Delivery of airtime and data bundles you buy
  • Cloudflare - Hosting of our web pages, and checks against automated abuse on public payment pages
  • Anthropic - Powers Taya, the in-app assistant. Only the request you make and the group records needed to answer it are sent, and they are not used to train Anthropic's models
  • Celcom Africa - Our SMS delivery partner for bulk notifications
  • Resend - Transactional email delivery, including account verification and two-factor authentication codes
  • Google LLC - For Google Sign-In authentication

We may disclose your data to law enforcement or regulatory authorities if required to do so by Kenyan law or a valid court order.

6. Member Data and Organisation Responsibility

When an administrator registers member data (names, phone numbers, financial records) on GroupYetu360, that administrator and their organisation are the data controllers for that member data. EPH Technologies Limited acts as the data processor.

Organisations using GroupYetu360 are responsible for:

  • Obtaining consent from members before registering their personal data
  • Ensuring member data is accurate and up to date
  • Informing members that their data is managed on GroupYetu360

7. Your Rights (Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019)

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, you have the right to:

  • Access - Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction - Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion - Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Objection - Object to certain processing of your data
  • Portability - Request your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@groupyetu.org. We will respond within 21 days as required by law.

8. Payment Data

When you pay through GroupYetu360 (a group contribution, a Michango gift, an event ticket or airtime):

  • Your M-Pesa phone number is used to send you the payment prompt
  • The M-Pesa confirmation (receipt number, amount, date and name) is stored to record your payment
  • Your M-Pesa PIN is entered on Safaricom's own system and is never sent to or stored by GroupYetu360
  • The money is received into EPH Technologies' M-Pesa paybill and then sent on to the group's approved paybill, till or number, or held for the Michango or event organiser until they withdraw it - see our Terms of Service for details
  • Payment data is also processed under Safaricom's own privacy policy

9. Cookies and Tracking

GroupYetu360 uses only essential cookies required for authentication and session management. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics services that track you across websites.

10. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account or organisation account is active on GroupYetu360. If an organisation account is deleted, all associated member and financial data is permanently deleted within 30 days. You may request deletion at any time.

Identity photos are deleted when they are no longer needed:

  • If your identity is not approved: 90 days after the decision
  • If you start but never send a verification: after 30 days
  • If your identity is verified: one year after you last needed it, that is one year after the later of your verification, your last Michango withdrawal and the last time you were an official of a group collecting by M-Pesa. While you run a live Michango or hold such a role, the photos are kept

We keep a record of the decision (your name, ID number and the dates) after the photos are deleted, so that we can show who was verified. Records of payments are kept as long as the law requires for financial records.

11. Children's Privacy

GroupYetu360 is not intended for use by persons under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, please contact us immediately.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered organisation administrators by email of any material changes. The updated policy will be published at groupyetu.org/privacy.html with the effective date.
