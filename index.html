Questions groups ask us

Is there an app?

Yes. GroupYetu360 is on Google Play for Android phones. On iPhone, open app.groupyetu.org in Safari and choose "Add to Home Screen" for the same app-like experience. It also works in any browser on a computer or tablet.

How do M-Pesa payments work?

Members tap Pay, choose what they are paying for and receive an M-Pesa prompt on their phone. They enter their PIN with Safaricom, and the payment is recorded against their name automatically. GroupYetu360 never sees anyone's PIN. Before instant payments open for a group, we verify the paybill, till or number the group's money is sent on to.

Can members see each other's balances?

No. Each member sees only their own payments, balances and welfare history. Admins and treasurers see the whole group, and you decide who holds those roles.

Is our group's money and data safe?

Your records are encrypted in storage and in transit, and each group can only ever see its own data. Payments go through Safaricom, and every change in the books is logged. We comply with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

We already keep records in Excel. Can we bring them in?

Yes. You can import your member list, and our team can help move your existing contributions and balances across so you start with complete records.

How does bulk SMS work?

Buy SMS credits in bundles from within the app whenever you need them. Credits never expire. Send reminders to everyone, to active members, or only to those behind on payments.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Your group returns to the Starter limits unless you choose a plan. Nothing is charged automatically, your records are kept, and you get reminders before the trial ends.
