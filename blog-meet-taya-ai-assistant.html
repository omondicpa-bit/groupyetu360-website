Meet Taya: Your Group's New AI Assistant

Taya lives inside GroupYetu360. It writes your meeting minutes, explains your finances in plain language, and drafts arrears reminders. All in seconds, not hours.

GroupYetu360 Team · Updated 2026

Every group has the same quiet burden. Someone has to write up what happened at the meeting. Someone has to explain the numbers to members who weren't there. Someone has to send the awkward reminder to the member who hasn't paid in two months. That work usually falls on one person, late at night, after everyone else has gone home.

Taya is built to take that work off your plate. It's an AI assistant that lives inside GroupYetu360, connected to your group's real records. Not a separate app. Not something you copy and paste numbers into. It already knows your group.

What Taya actually does

Meeting minutes, done in seconds

Tell Taya what was discussed. It writes clean, formatted minutes ready to file. No more typing them up from memory the next morning.

Plain language financial summaries

Ask Taya for this month's numbers. You get a clear breakdown in normal language. Not a spreadsheet you have to decode yourself.

Arrears reminders that sound like you

Taya drafts a polite, direct nudge for members who are behind on contributions. You review it, approve it, and it goes out. The awkward conversation gets easier.

Instant answers about your group

Ask Taya something like "when did Fredrick last pay?" It checks your actual records and answers right away. No digging through transaction history yourself.

A real example. An admin asks Taya to remind members who haven't paid this month. Taya finds three people in arrears, drafts a short SMS, and asks for approval before sending. One message, three members reminded, no manual lookup required.

Why this matters for a volunteer-run group

Most chama, SACCO, and welfare group leadership is unpaid. Treasurers and chairpersons do this work on top of their actual jobs. The admin tasks that eat up their time are rarely the interesting part of running a group. They're the paperwork.

Taya doesn't replace the judgment calls a real admin has to make. It handles the repetitive, time consuming parts so the people running your group can spend their limited time on decisions that actually need a human.

What Taya doesn't do

Taya doesn't move money, approve payments, or make decisions on the group's behalf. It drafts, summarizes, and answers. A person still reviews and approves anything that goes out, like an SMS to members. That's a deliberate choice, not a limitation we're working around.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Taya available on every plan?

Taya is built into GroupYetu360 and accessible to group admins. Usage is designed to stay sustainable across all plans, including the free Starter tier.

Does Taya see our group's private financial data?

Taya only accesses the records of the group asking the question, the same data an admin already sees inside their own dashboard. It doesn't have visibility into other organisations on the platform.

Can Taya send messages without anyone checking them first?

No. Taya drafts and asks for approval before anything is sent to members. A human always makes the final call.
