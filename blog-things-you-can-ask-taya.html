5 Things You Can Ask Taya Right Now

Taya has grown since we first introduced it. Here's what it actually handles today, with real examples you can try on your own group.

GroupYetu360 Team · Updated 2026

When we first introduced Taya, it handled two things well. Meeting minutes and financial summaries. Since then, it's grown quietly in the background. Today it answers a much wider range of real questions admins actually ask. Here are five, with the kind of question you'd actually type.

1. "When did [member] last pay?"

Taya checks that member's actual transaction history and answers directly. No scrolling through records yourself. No asking the treasurer to look it up for you.

2. "Who hasn't paid this month, and can you remind them?"

Taya finds the members in arrears, drafts a short reminder, and shows it to you before anything goes out. Approve it, and it sends. This is a real send now, not just a draft you copy elsewhere.

3. "How much do we have in the welfare fund?"

Taya answers questions about specific pots, not just the group's overall balance. Welfare, table banking, rotating savings, and general contributions can all be asked about separately.

4. "Who's up next in the rotation?"

For groups running a merry-go-round or table banking cycle, Taya can tell you where the rotation stands without you checking a spreadsheet or asking around.

5. "Write up the minutes from today's meeting"

Still the original use case, and still one of the most useful. Describe what happened, and Taya turns it into clean, formatted minutes ready to file.

One thing that hasn't changed. Taya still doesn't send anything without you approving it first. It drafts. You decide. That was true when we launched it, and it's still true now that it can do more.

Why this matters more than it looks like

None of these five questions is dramatic on its own. That's the point. Running a group well is mostly small, repetitive questions. Who paid. Who didn't. What's the balance. What happened at the last meeting. Answering these used to mean interrupting the treasurer or digging through a notebook. Now it means typing a question and getting a real answer, from real data, in seconds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to learn special commands to use Taya?

No. Ask in plain language, the way you'd ask a person. Taya is built to understand normal questions, not specific syntax.

Can regular members use Taya, or only admins?

Taya is currently built for group admins, since most of what it handles, arrears reminders, financial summaries, involves information admins are responsible for managing.

What happens if Taya doesn't know the answer?

It says so directly rather than guessing. Taya only answers from your group's real records.
